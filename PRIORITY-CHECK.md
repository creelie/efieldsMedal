# Priority check, 10 October 2026

Checked at about 17:25 UTC on 10 October 2026 whether anyone had decided Tran's Conjecture 8.6
(arXiv:2609.06553, "four equal cross edges for p-secting planes") before this work.

| Source | What was found |
|---|---|
| arXiv:2609.06553v3 (Tran, updated 6 October 2026), the source of the conjecture | Conjecture 8.6 is still stated as open. Section 18.5 says the methods were not applied to parameters other than e = f = g = 3, and Problem 8 of Section 19 asks to "decide Conjecture 8.6". |
| arXiv:2610.01216v1 (Tran, "Morley Simplices in Higher Dimensions: Regularity, Reflections, and Counterexamples", 1 October 2026) | About trisecting hyperplanes of n-simplices only. The full text never mentions p-secting planes or Conjecture 8.6. |
| arXiv search (Morley with tetrahedron, simplex or simplices, newest first) | No other paper on the converse Morley problem after 2609.06553. |
| Zenodo search ("Morley tetrahedron", "p-secting", "Tran Morley conjecture") | No record on the topic. |
| Web search ("p-secting", "Conjecture 8.6", Morley tetrahedron) | Only Tran's own preprint and older work on the planar theorem and on isosceles tetrahedra (Tran, Acta Math. Hungar. 2021). |

The conjecture was open when this work was done. Tran's preprint proves the conjecture for e = f = g = 3 on
isosceles tetrahedra (Theorems 10.1 and 10.3), which the paper cites; the counterexamples here need
parameters that are not all equal, and for e = f = g = 4 the paper shows that only the regular
isosceles tetrahedron has a regular Morley tetrahedron.
